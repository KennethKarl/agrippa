Frequently asked questions

Where do I order and pay?

Ordering, payment, and shipping are handled on the Naver store. Use the “Buy on Naver” button on each page. (Amazon is coming soon.)

Can I customize colors and quantity?

Yes. Each piece is handmade, so you can request the number of carnations or roses, color combinations, ribbons, and more. Leave a note through the store.

How long does it last?

It needs no water and never wilts. Kept away from direct sunlight and moisture, it holds its shape and color for years.

How do I care for it?

Do not water it. Dust it with a soft brush or the cool setting of a hair dryer, and keep it indoors.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — gift wrapping for Parents’ Day, coming-of-age, graduations, and more can be prepared. Just ask when ordering.

How long does shipping take?

Because pieces are handmade, production takes time. For a specific date, we recommend ordering at least 1–2 weeks ahead. Check the store for exact timing.

Can I get reminders on KakaoTalk?

Add our KakaoTalk channel to receive “prepare ahead” reminders before occasions like Parents’ Day and coming-of-age day.
