What is a chenille (pipe cleaner) flower?

A chenille flower — “moru” flower in Korean — is a flower crafted by hand, one bloom at a time, from chenille stems (pipe cleaners). Unlike fresh flowers, it never wilts, making it an everlasting handmade gift.

What is it made of?

A “moru” is a thin wire wrapped in soft chenille fiber. The wire is bent into petals, a calyx, and a stem. The wire core holds the shape, while the soft surface gives a lifelike texture.

How long does it last?

It needs no water and never wilts. Kept away from direct sunlight and moisture, it holds its shape and color for years — essentially an everlasting gift.

How is it different from fresh or artificial flowers?

  • Fresh flowers: beautiful, but wilt within days and need water.
  • Artificial (plastic/silk): last long, but are mass-produced with little character.
  • Chenille flowers: last long and are handmade one by one, so colors and arrangements are customizable.

How do you care for it?

Do not water it. Dust it off with a soft brush or the cool setting of a hair dryer. Keep it indoors and away from prolonged direct sunlight to prevent fading.

What occasions is it good for?

It fits date-driven occasions especially well — carnations for Parents’ Day, roses for coming-of-age and anniversaries, bouquets for graduations and birthdays — because it can be prepared in advance without wilting.
